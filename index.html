How Brew & Bite CEC works

  1. Choose your items. Browse the menu or use the order calculator to see your total.
  2. Send a pre-order. Fill in the pre-order form, then send the summary to us on Messenger.
  3. Pick up near campus. Collect your drink and cookie at your chosen time.

Why we started

Our campus survey of 31 students showed strong interest in affordable iced coffee and in ordering ahead through Messenger. Brew & Bite CEC is built around those two answers.
